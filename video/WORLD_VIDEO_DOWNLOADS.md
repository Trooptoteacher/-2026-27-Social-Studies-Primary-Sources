# World History — Downloadable Public-Domain Video (clickable)

Real, clickable links to **download** public-domain World History video and self-host it.
Rights flags: **✅ PD (US federal work)** = guaranteed public domain, commercial-safe;
**⚠️ verify** = likely PD but confirm the item's rights box before hosting (foreign-origin
or commercial-newsreel/educational-film provenance). Cite the **originating institution**,
not the aggregator (see the how-to). Wikimedia items are **discovery-only** — trace to the
origin. Download steps: **`HOW-TO-DOWNLOAD-VIDEOS.md`**.

> Pre-modern World (ancient/medieval) has essentially **no authentic PD video** — carry
> those standards with the primary-source image/map bank. PD video is strong ~1914→present.

## Best-practice picks (all ✅ guaranteed PD, US federal works)
Start here — no per-item gamble: **Flashes of Action** (WWI), the **NARA WWI Films**,
**D-Day 1944**, **News of the Day 1937–43**, **WWII National Archives videos**, and the
**War Dept. Military Intelligence** reel. These are U.S.-government productions (public
domain under 17 U.S.C. §105), commercial-safe, and cite cleanly to NARA.
