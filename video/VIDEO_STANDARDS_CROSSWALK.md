# World Video → Standards Crosswalk

Grade 8 (US-to-Reconstruction), grades 6–7 (ancient/medieval), and Government don't correlate with world-war content. Codes are era-level correlations drawn from the verbatim 2026-27 standards text — confirm the final per-clip mapping against the exact footage you pull.
